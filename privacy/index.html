Privacy policy

Effective November 3, 2026

Davoid is a local notebook developed by Chengkai Xiang. This policy covers the Davoid app and this website.

Your notes and attachments

Your writing, drawings, images and other attachments are stored on your device. Davoid does not send your notes, attachments, usage history or device identifiers to the developer. The app has no account system, advertising or third-party behavioral analytics.

Files you choose

Davoid accesses files, photos and folders that you select or add. Files and folder copies imported into a note are included when you export an editable project. For a linked folder, Davoid stores read-access authorization on that device. That authorization is not included in an exported project.

Exports and other services

You choose where to save or share projects, PDFs and attachments. If you use iCloud Drive, another file provider or a sharing service, that service handles the files under its own privacy policy. Opening a web link from a note also subjects the visit to the destination website’s privacy practices.

Storage and control

Notes are kept in Davoid’s local application storage. Exported files and backups remain in the locations where you or your backup services save them. To remove exported copies, delete them from those locations. Contact support if you need help locating or removing Davoid’s local data.

Support requests

If you choose to contact the developer, the information you provide is used to respond to your request and investigate the issue. Send only the information needed for support. Support email is received through Apple iCloud Mail. Your email address, message and any attachments you send are processed by the mail services involved in delivery and retained as needed to handle your request. We do not use support messages for advertising. See Apple’s Privacy Policy for information about Apple’s services. You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing 06builds_anagram@icloud.com.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no advertising, analytics scripts or third-party embedded media. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details about its processing.

Changes and questions

This policy may be updated when Davoid’s features or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version. For privacy questions, visit the support page.
